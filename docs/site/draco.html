Draco research

DWARFLAB Android app evidence and vendor specifications for the Draco Standard and SHO editions. DwarfAlp has no Draco device profile, and this project has no Draco hardware verification.

November 2026 OTA and Android update

On 26 November 2026 the production OTA service offered Draco application firmware 1.1.0.134 for model 6 when the request reported system build 20261111a. With the system rollback index unset (0), the service offered 20261111a first and identified 1.1.0.134 as the target application version. An up-to-date 1.1.0.134 request returned no package. The application update record was last updated on 25 November. The public release notes still list Draco firmware 1.1.0.96 and mobile app 3.4.5. DWARFLAB Download Center

The 1.1.0.134 OTA notes announce flat-frame capture, a 2:1 default SHO imaging ratio, and a fix for dark-frame captures that stalled during saving. The notes give no ordering for the two components of the ratio. The earlier 1.1.0.96 notes describe guide-status notifications, stacked PNG and FITS exports, automatic dark frames and HDR controls, SHO edge cropping, and fixes for field derotation, scheduling, and stacking. November 2026 app settings and vendor specifications

The project’s 23 November 2026 app UI notes record Astro and HO for Standard, and Astro, HO, SO, and SHO for SHO Edition. Both lists showed 30, 45, 60, 90, 120, 180, 240, 300, and 500 s, with gain 0, 2, 5, then 10–240 in steps of 10. The notes leave the app build unspecified. DWARFLAB advertises 300 s single-frame exposures. DWARFLAB product specifications

DWARFLAB lists an Astro filter, a dark position, and Hα + O III (HO) for both editions. Standard has a built-in ND solar filter. SHO Edition adds S II + O III (SO) and uses an external ND solar filter. DWARFLAB describes SHO as a combination of data captured through the two dual-band filters. The APK values above describe logical app choices. Physical positions and firmware acceptance remain unverified. DWARFLAB Draco guide

DWARFLAB specifies a 90 mm aperture and 340 mm physical focal length for the tele camera, approximately 12 MP deep-sky output with 2×2 binning, a separate wide camera, and a monochrome OS04C1B guide camera. DWARFLAB product specifications · DWARFLAB Draco guide

Model-specific app workflows in 3.4.1

  • Imaging controls. DeviceType exposes wide focus, solar ND switching, lens defog, automatic cooling, horizontal calibration, and Alt-Az deep-sky mosaic for the Draco model. The generic ND switch gate does not encode the edition-specific hardware.
  • Preview geometry. The Draco CameraInfo presets in DeviceType use 1920 × 1080 for both cameras, with fields of view of 1.872° × 1.055° for tele and 70.1° × 43.2° for wide. These are historical app presets. Draco FITS dimensions and fields of view need measurement.
  • EQ alignment. The capture UI has Draco-specific safety and guiding status handling. CaptureViewModel retains the optional calibration choice for Draco when starting EQ solving. DracoEqModeStatePolicy keeps a running-solve event from replacing app state 4, 5, 6, 7, or 9.
  • Atlas schedules. ScheduleDetailFragment lists Draco among the plan models. For Draco atlas tasks with mosaicType=1, ScheduleTaskRotationPolicy uses the Draco mapping in MosaicFovInfo. It clamps the angle to −90°…90°, wraps negative values by adding 360°, and encodes the result in hundredths of a degree. A missing frame uses zero. Schedule upload, execution, and rotation on Draco firmware remain unverified.
