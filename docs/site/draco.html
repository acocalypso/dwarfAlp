Draco research

DWARFLAB Android app evidence and vendor specifications for the Draco Standard and SHO editions. DwarfAlp has no Draco device profile, and this project has no Draco hardware verification.

APK camera parameters

DWARFLAB 3.4.1 declares DeviceType.DRACO with model ID 6. This ID identifies the app model. Draco’s response envelope and firmware protocol version remain unverified.

The base APKs for 3.4.1 and 3.4.3 contain the following model-6 entries in assets/params_range.json. Exposure limits refer to a sparse lookup table.

November 2026 app settings and vendor specifications

The project’s 23 November 2026 app UI notes record Astro and HO for Standard, and Astro, HO, SO, and SHO for SHO Edition. Both lists showed 30, 45, 60, 90, 120, 180, 240, 300, and 500 s, with the gain steps above. The notes leave the app build unspecified. The 500 s choice is a UI observation only. DWARFLAB advertises 300 s single-frame exposures, and the two inspected APK tables stop at 300 s tele. DWARFLAB product specifications

DWARFLAB lists an Astro filter, a dark position, and Hα + O III (HO) for both editions. Standard has a built-in ND solar filter. SHO Edition adds S II + O III (SO) and uses an external ND solar filter. DWARFLAB describes SHO as a combination of data captured through the two dual-band filters. The command and filter index mapping for the newer choices remains unverified. DWARFLAB Draco guide

DWARFLAB specifies a 90 mm aperture and 340 mm physical focal length for the tele camera, approximately 12 MP deep-sky output with 2×2 binning, a separate wide camera, and a monochrome OS04C1B guide camera. DWARFLAB product specifications · DWARFLAB Draco guide

Model-specific app workflows in 3.4.1

  • Imaging controls. DeviceType exposes wide focus, solar ND switching, lens defog, automatic cooling, horizontal calibration, and Alt-Az deep-sky mosaic for the Draco model. The generic ND switch gate does not encode the edition-specific hardware.
  • Preview geometry. The Draco CameraInfo presets in DeviceType use 1920 × 1080 for both cameras, with fields of view of 1.872° × 1.055° for tele and 70.1° × 43.2° for wide. These are historical app presets. Draco FITS dimensions and fields of view need measurement.
  • EQ alignment. The capture UI has Draco-specific safety and guiding status handling. CaptureViewModel retains the optional calibration choice for Draco when starting EQ solving. DracoEqModeStatePolicy keeps a running-solve event from replacing app state 4, 5, 6, 7, or 9.
  • Atlas schedules. ScheduleDetailFragment lists Draco among the plan models. For Draco atlas tasks with mosaicType=1, ScheduleTaskRotationPolicy uses the Draco mapping in MosaicFovInfo. It clamps the angle to −90°…90°, wraps negative values by adding 360°, and encodes the result in hundredths of a degree. A missing frame uses zero. Schedule upload, execution, and rotation on Draco firmware remain unverified.
